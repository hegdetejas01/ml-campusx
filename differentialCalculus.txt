# Differentiation
    It is the process of finding the derivative of a function. The derivative of a function represents the instantaneous rate of change of the function wrt its variable

    Rate of Change : 
        if y = f(x) = x^2
        if x is increased by delta x, then by what value y changes
        rate of change = (f(x+deltax) - f(x)) / deltax

    Instantaneous rate of change : 
        instantaneous means at a single point
        that is delta x moves towards ZERO (very small)
        instantaneous rate of change = (f(x+dx) - f(x)) / dx : this is derivative
        df(x)/dx = (f(x+dx) - f(x)) / dx

    Population growth rate, velocity etc are measured for a time interval and not at a point.
    Eg: what is the population growth rate at 1990 can't be measured,  
        what is the velocity at time t=4 can't be calculated

        To do this, we should have the difference between intervals very small (like dx) to measure at that point. This is done by differentiation.

    When the difference is almost zero, they represent a same point and derivative at that point gives the slope at that point. 
    df/dx is the slope at that point


    # Intution of derivatives
        y = f(x) = x^2
        
        dy/dx represents by what quantity y=x^2 changes when x changes by a small value
        if, dy/dx = -2, then a small change in value of x changes the value of y by -2 times

        Eg: dL/dw0 means by what value L changes when there is a small change in w0

# Sum Rule: 
    (f(x) + g(x))' = f'(x) + g'(x)
    (f(x) - g(x))' = f'(x) - g'(x)

# Product Rule:
    (f(x) * g(x))' = (f'(x) * g(x)) + (f(x) * g'(x))
    (cf(x))' = c * f'(x)
